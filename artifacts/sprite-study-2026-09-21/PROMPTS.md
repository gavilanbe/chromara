## carmin — overworld

Use case: style-transfer
Asset type: four-direction OVERWORLD sprite visual test for Chromara. This is a sheet of small exploration sprites, seen with a slightly elevated top-down JRPG camera, distinctly simpler and more compact than battle sprites.
Input image 1: the chosen character's already established unarmed combat sprite-sheet. It is a character identity and palette reference only; REDESIGN the rendering as coherent compact overworld sprites. Preserve personality, colors, distinctive silhouette, beret/apex/crest as specified, but simplify small details to read well while walking around a world map.
Pixel technique: excellent hand-drawn 2D game pixel art, each actual sprite about 32–40 source pixels tall, visibly enlarged with clean square nearest-neighbor pixels. Consistent single pixel lattice and uniform pixel size in all 16 frames. Selective dark colored outlines, small economical pixel clusters, controlled hue-shifted ramps of 6–10 colors, tiny clean specular highlights, reduced readable faces. No watercolor, brush texture, noise, dither noise, antialiasing, smooth gradients, blur, vectors, 3D or voxels. Do NOT simply shrink the battle sheet: design a proper slightly top-down map version, with the top of the head/beret visible, broad readable body, compact appendages.
Layout: EXACT FOUR columns and FOUR rows, SIXTEEN isolated full-body sprites on a quiet FLAT SOLID PALE-IVORY #f7f2e8 background, landscape sheet. Identical evenly sized invisible cells and generous empty gutters. One centered character in each cell, consistent bottom-center origin. All sprites use the same scale, proportions and upper-left light. No outlines around cells, NO text, NO numbers, NO gridlines, NO panels, NO decorations, NO props, NO floor shadows, NO landscape. Every sprite fully contained within its cell.
Completely EMPTY HANDS in every cell: no weapons, brushes, held pencils, palettes, bags or equipment. Keep the intrinsic character details as specified. Small rounded pigment nubs instead of articulated human fingers. No new costume. Retain the cute expressive flowing pigment body.

Directions for the FIRST THREE rows (same order in each row):
COLUMN 1: SOUTH, front facing viewer, both eyes visible.
COLUMN 2: WEST, true LEFT-facing profile, nose and gaze point to the LEFT edge of the image.
COLUMN 3: EAST, true RIGHT-facing profile, nose and gaze point to the RIGHT edge of the image.
COLUMN 4: NORTH, FULL REAR VIEW facing away toward top of the image, symmetrical back of body, NO eyes or mouth visible anywhere. Show the BACK of the distinguishing character feature, not a second front face.

ROW 1: IDLE normal upright neutral pose in all four directions.
ROW 2: MOVEMENT CONTACT, body gently compressed/wider, low stance with tiny nubs shifting, in all four directions. These are four frames of the SAME type of contact pose, one in each direction.
ROW 3: MOVEMENT HOP, lifted and slightly taller/narrower, tiny nubs tucked, in all four directions. These are four frames of the SAME airborne pose, one in each direction. The hop should preserve body mass and identity.
ROW 4: FOUR FRONTAL SPECIAL REACTIONS, all SOUTH-facing and same compact map perspective: [column 1] idle BLINK with horizontal closed eyelids and calm mouth; [column 2] SURPRISED / startled, big wide open eyes and a small O mouth, body tensed; [column 3] SQUASHED HIT reaction, body compressed downward, tightly shut wincing eyes and pained mouth; [column 4] KO, low flattened puddle-shaped body with crossed or closed eyes, character still recognizable.

Do not add extra drops, effect marks, hearts, stars, text bubbles or sweat icons. Only Añil's three intrinsic satellite drops are permitted when Añil is the selected character. Most important: correct directions (back has NO FACE), empty hands, readable tiny sprite design, a CONSISTENT character in all 16 cells.
Chosen character:
ONLY CARMÍN, the red character from the reference. Sturdy short broad round living drop of red paint, vivid carmine #e23c3c, red/plum shadows, warm red highlights, determined but warm expressive face. His dark wine/aubergine painter's beret with tiny top stalk MUST remain on in ALL sixteen poses, including rear views and flattened KO. Compact rounded pigment nubs as empty hands and low soft feet, no human anatomy. His body is broad and plump, not tapered, and he has no long tail. Keep beret orientation and mass consistent when turning.

### Ajuste final

Use case: precise-object-edit
Input image 1 is the edit target, a sixteen-frame 4 by 4 pixel-art sprite sheet. Replace ONLY the dark colored gradient background and all glow/halos behind the sprites with a perfectly FLAT SOLID uniform pale ivory #f7f2e8 background across the entire canvas, corner to corner. Preserve all sixteen sprites, their exact positions, pixel shapes, colors, sizes, details, expressions, and empty hands. Remove diffuse halo pixels outside the sprites, but retain hard pixel sprite contours and every intrinsic character part. DO NOT put any shadows, gradients or texture on the new background. It must be a flat neutral sprite-review background, like blank paper filled with a single RGB color. Do not crop, rearrange, add text or change the layout.
